# BeforeRun branding

The BeforeRun identity combines three ideas:

- **Shield:** static inspection and containment before trust.
- **Terminal prompt:** developer workflows and repository execution surfaces.
- **Controlled flame:** dangerous behavior detected before it spreads.

## Assets

| Asset | Intended use |
| --- | --- |
| `assets/brand/beforerun-mark.svg` | App icon, package icon, social avatar |
| `assets/brand/beforerun-lockup.svg` | README, documentation, and release pages |

Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least one quarter of its width. Do not rotate, skew, recolor, or place the logo over visually noisy backgrounds.
